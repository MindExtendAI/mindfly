MUSE EEG AND FOCUS
Muse connection uses muse-js 3.3.0 (MIT): https://github.com/urish/muse-js
The EEG signal processing and focus implementation belongs to MindExtend AI LLC.
It is distributed as a proprietary Rust/WebAssembly binary.
See licenses/EEG-BINARY-NOTICE.txt.
Bluetooth, waveforms and device-specific calibration storage run in the browser.
Raw EEG is not uploaded. Focus is experimental feedback, not a validated test of
attention. Signal loss, missing calibration or engine failure stops control.
The neural simulation also runs in Rust/WASM without a JavaScript fallback.

XENOVA ANIMATION CODE
Four source modules are vendored unchanged from the pinned Xenova revision
listed above: src/fly-appearance.js, src/wings.js, src/gait.js, src/body/fk.js.
MIT license: /fly/XENOVA-LICENSE.txt. Forward kinematics, leg IK, folded wing
poses, procedural surface detail and bristles support the walking visualization.
MindFly uses its own walking controller and layout.

BPN WALKING MODEL
LIF dynamics adapted from blendi-remade/fly-brain-minecraft, commit
6cfa30175003ef25da68a237d5eda958f8047b82 (MIT; licenses/fly-brain-minecraft.txt).
MaleCNS anatomy and connectivity: Janelia FlyEM male-cns:v1.0, CC BY 4.0.
Study: Bidaye et al. 2020, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2020.07.032.
54-cell stimulus-specific induced subgraph, 24 partial BPN candidates.
Human EEG gating, walking direction and six-leg animation are authored mappings;
no motor-neuron-to-muscle, force, or biological alpha-wave validation is claimed.
